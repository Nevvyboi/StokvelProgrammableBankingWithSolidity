THE Q4 STOKVEL, CONSTITUTION, VERSION 1

1. Six members. Each pays the agreed contribution into the club's Investec account every month.
2. Every month the whole pot goes to one member, in the order of the member numbers, 01 to 06, then again from 01.
3. Nobody chooses the recipient. The order is the order.
4. The club pays out only when the bank has shown, within the last hour, that it holds everything the club is owed.
5. Any change to the contribution, a member's card, the relayer or the attestor takes a majority of members and a waiting period before it applies.
6. A member who loses their card asks the others to move their membership to a spare card. Their history stays with them.
7. The record of all of this lives on a public chain, where any member can read it and nobody can quietly rewrite it.
